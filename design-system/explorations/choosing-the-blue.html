One hue (262°) held constant; only lightness steps, with chroma tapered at both ends (pale tints and deep shades can't hold high chroma without turning muddy or neon). 600 = the button, 500 = the interactive/hover brighten, 50–100 = soft fills and tinted chips, 700–900 = text-on-tint and pressed states. Your ink and surfaces are this same hue at ~2% chroma — that's why the whole thing already coheres.

Verdict. Keep blue, keep the hue neighborhood — but move the primary off the hot SaaS value to a deeper, slightly calmer blue (~#0B57CE / oklch .50 .17 262). It clears AA with real headroom, reads like an encouraging coach rather than a checkout button, and generates a clean, coherent ramp that your existing neutrals already belong to. The current bright blue becomes the hover/brighten, not the resting state.
