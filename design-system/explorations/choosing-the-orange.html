The right orange

Orange isn't chosen for meaning like blue was — it's chosen by relationship (the complement of the locked primary) and by one job (the warm spark), then constrained hard by contrast. Here's the reasoning made visible.

500 is the accent; 50–200 are the soft peach fills (with dark ink); 700–900 are the AA-safe "orange text" browns. Same discipline as the blue ramp — one hue, lightness does the work.

Verdict. A friendly true orange at ~#E8742E (oklch .70 .15 52) — the complement of your blue, spaced clear of both critical-red and warning-amber. Its job is the single warm spark: brand moments and encouragement, always as a tint fill with dark text or a small solid dot — never a white-text button, never a body-text color unless darkened to the ~700 brown.
