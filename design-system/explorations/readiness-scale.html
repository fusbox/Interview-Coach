Trade-off: the monochrome ramp is calmer and even simpler to govern — one hue, lightness only — but the four steps sit closer together, so it leans harder on the labels to be read at a glance. The botanical version buys more separation (and more meaning) for the cost of a second hue in the state family.

Recommendation. The botanical progression — slate → yellow-green → viridian → deep brand green. It's the most legible at a glance, it turns the scale into the product's own growth story, and it lands "Strong" precisely on the TalentArbor green. Orange stays out of it (rare spark), red never appears. Per the accessibility foundation, every state also carries its label and dot, so the color is never doing the work alone.
