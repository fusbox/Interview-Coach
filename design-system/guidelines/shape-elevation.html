Elevation is hierarchy, not decoration

Elevation combines border, shadow, surface and state. Shadows are earned: they separate a working object from its canvas, indicate interaction, or lift an overlay above the page.

Primitive depth ladder

--shadow-flatBoundary only. Rows, tables, quiet controls.
--shadow-raised-1Default contained card depth.
--shadow-raised-2Candidate widget or hover lift.
--shadow-floatingDialogs, popovers, drawers and sheets.
--shadow-primary-glowShort neutral CTA shadow with a blue tint.

Semantic elevation roles

Object role
Token
Use
Row
--elevation-row
Tables, compact admin rows, filter rows. Border-led, not shadow-led.
Card
--elevation-card
Default contained cards that need separation from the canvas.
Widget
--elevation-widget
Candidate dashboard widgets and action surfaces with stronger visual priority.
Panel
--elevation-panel
Large candidate setup, debrief/report or explanatory surfaces.
Overlay
--elevation-overlay
Dialogs, popovers, sheets, drawers and floating inspectors.
Primary action
--elevation-cta
Short neutral-first drop shadow with a restrained blue tint. Do not use on generic cards or selected controls.
Hover / pressed
--elevation-hover / --elevation-pressed
Interactive surfaces lift one tier on hover and settle down on press.
