Foundations · Color · Surface worksheet

Define the surface system before revamping classes

This worksheet starts from the product we want, not the classes we inherited. Existing classes are evidence to reconcile after the desired route contexts, surface roles, and interaction rules are clear.

Route Contexts

Each context can share primitives, but it should not share the same surface mix. A protected recruiter workspace and a candidate coaching dashboard should feel related, not identical.

Public marketingCurrent public routes are root and /recruiter. One landing page may funnel candidates and clients to the right host-platform destination.
Recruiter protectedDense, operational, repeatable. Optimized for scanning and action.
Candidate protectedSelf-regulated learning mediated by feedforward and feedback indicators.
Session modeShared candidate experience across recruiter and candidate apps; focused, low-distraction, answer-first.

Reference Translation

Use external UI references as craft cues, not as palette or layout sources. The useful pattern is confidence: compact controls, careful spacing, distinct surface moods, and widgets that earn their chrome.

Compact launchersCoach update and next practice round can open from header/avatar-style controls. The opened panel is the larger surface; the launcher can stay small.
Intermediate canvasThe canvas can sit between light and rich surfaces, giving panels room to be either brighter or calmer than the page field.
Targeted glassKeep glass for overlays, sticky controls, docked launchers, and temporary focus. Avoid making it the default material.
Mood budgetPalette saturation changes the product mood. Candidate dashboard defaults understated; milestone, action, or marketing moments can be livelier.
Widget cardsDashboard cards may carry internal indicators: status pills, progress rings, category chips, mini charts, or current-plan badges.
Learning viewsQuestion set, category, skill, feedback, and practice guidance should read as one learning system, not unrelated card types.

Decision Notes

Marketing latitudeRoot and /recruiter are the current public routes. If preserved, one landing page should use CTAs to direct current and potential candidates or clients to the right host-platform destination.
Candidate dashboard rangeTake no cues from current UI. Open coach update and next practice from compact launchers; let the opened content use coach, insight, widget, sheet, or overlay surfaces.
Widget boundaryWidget cards can own internal indicators such as status pills, progress rings, chips, or mini charts without becoming new surface roles.
Canvas inheritanceCanvas can be an intermediate value so surfaces may sit lighter or richer than the page field; decide when a role may introduce its own canvas-level treatment.
Glass boundaryGlass is targeted overlay/control material, not a true system surface or default card material.
Brand tint boundaryBrand tint can appear inside protected surfaces when it serves the design.
Nesting ruleOpen exploration. No firm rule yet; decide from real layouts rather than inherited card structure.
Dark theme readinessAll surface roles need direct dark counterparts before implementation.
