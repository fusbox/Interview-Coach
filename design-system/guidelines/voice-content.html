Voice

A calm, encouraging coach.

The product speaks as "the coach" — first person, to "you", the candidate. Never judgmental, never gamified-cutesy. Encouraging and low-pressure.

How the coach speaks

"Let's get you ready for your interview."

"You'll answer a series of interview-style questions tailored to your target role."

Dashboard, first person: "What I noticed" → "Add this to my next round."

Micro-guidance is verb-first

"Use the next round to add clearer details, rationale, or outcomes."

"Practice one answer with a clear beginning, middle, and ending."

"No time limit — take your time. Thoughtful answers lead to better feedback."

Casing

Sentence case for body and most headings — "Preparedness map", "Question plan", "Current read".

Title Case for short feature headings and CTAs — "Create Practice", "Private Coaching Feedback".

ALL-CAPS eyebrows via CSS — Coach Plan, Coach Update, Question set.

Trust & emoji

State privacy plainly, not in legalese: "Your answers are protected by access controls and are not shared with recruiters or employers for hiring decisions."

Emoji appear only in the 5-point feedback scale — never in copy.
