No advisory is attributed to `busboy`, `mammoth`, or `pdf-parse`. All three still require a tested framework/transitive upgrade or explicit temporary risk acceptance before pilot. Do not take npm's regressive Next downgrade or an untested transitive override. Development-only findings should be refreshed separately without conflating them with deployed runtime exposure.
